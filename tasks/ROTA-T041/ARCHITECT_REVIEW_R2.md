## R1 FINDINGS — CLOSURE

### R1-1 — incomplete target vector vs TARGET-01 hierarchy
**CLOSED.**

The corrected brief now freezes the product hierarchy explicitly:
- if at least one participating/available LOCAL lacks `target_hours`, the target vector is incomplete;
- `TARGET-01` and target-equity do not rank candidates in that solve;
- target-independent spread minimization across participating LOCAL replaces the incomplete-target ranking;
- when targets are complete, existing TARGET-01/equity semantics remain unchanged;
- the same canonical `actual PRIMARY hours` calculation must feed either mode, so T041 may not introduce a second hours owner.

This closes the ambiguity that could otherwise preserve the old 4-target preference and reproduce `180/180/180/180/0` while nominally adding a fairness term.

### R1-2 — fresh WORKING scope too broad
**CLOSED.**

The corrected brief limits automatic regeneration to the proven C-04 case:
- current WORKING has **no Assignments**;
- persisted demands materially differ from demands generated by the current catalog;
- next PLAN creates a fresh WORKING through existing `create_schedule_version` semantics;
- the old WORKING remains unchanged in history;
- `replace_working_snapshot()` is explicitly forbidden for this repair;
- no DB fingerprint/new field is introduced;
- a WORKING containing any Assignment is outside T041 and requires a separate OWNER decision if encountered.

This avoids silently inventing migration/rebinding semantics for already-planned work.

### R1-3 — COVERAGE-01 overlap ownership
**CLOSED.**

The corrected brief now freezes the missing invariant:
- for simultaneous independent demands, `covers_demand_id` disambiguates which occurrence a PRIMARY services;
- actual interval geometry must still prove that the tagged demand is genuinely covered;
- one PRIMARY may not satisfy two simultaneous independent occurrences over the same time segment;
- geometry remains authoritative for the accepted T022 non-simultaneous/manual-spanning cases;
- T41-A13 prevents geometry from silently reallocating two PRIMARY assignments tagged to the same overlapping demand onto a second demand.

This is compatible with both the AUDIT-1 C-03 defect and T022's protection against malicious/incorrect lineage tags.
