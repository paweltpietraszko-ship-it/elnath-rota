R3-01 — one correction uses two different clocks
--------------------------------------------------

TRACE: brief sections 3 and 6 plus H1/H2 require one recorded_at captured once
for the atomic operation, with all-future effective_from = recorded_at.date().

OWNERSHIP: rota/application/manual_edit.py owns both the computed effective_from
and the CoordinatorAction written for this operation.

REPRO: audit_r3_repro.py::test_one_recorded_at_owns_future_correction_effective_date
fails on the exact SHA. apply_manual_correction reads _now() for effective_from,
but _with_manual_action_hook independently calls datetime.now() for recorded_at.
The deterministic reproduction persisted effective_from=2000-01-01 while its
own action recorded_at was 2026-09-11. In production the same defect is a real
midnight race: one correction can be assigned to the previous date while its
history says it was recorded on the next date.

Required narrow correction: capture recorded_at once in the owning operation
and reuse that exact value for classification/effective_from and the action
history. The repository-wide autouse fixture added to conftest.py must also be
removed or narrowed: it currently forces manual-edit time to year 2000 in every
test and lets legacy tests accept a result that contradicts H1/H2.
