## Method and limits

Keep three separate categories: (A) owner's explicitly recorded decisions; (B) current/historical code and test observations; (C) inferences needing verification. Every evidence item links to an immutable commit or fixed branch snapshot and gives file/lines or an exact commit. A document's `OWNER_CORRECTED` heading is documentary evidence of an attributed decision, not independent access to the original conversation. A successful existing test is evidence of its tested path only, not proof of entire workflow correctness. Do not infer chronology from a current file alone.

## Interim falsifiable hypothesis — not a verdict

The strongest observed process failure is **a locally acceptable change or repair that does not preserve a cross-feature owner invariant**: T058 R6 silently undid a human decision despite a successful replan, and R7 repaired that path for one assignment while leaving the two-component 24h variant broken. We have historical reproducible evidence that the independent auditor caught and the implementer subsequently fixed those bugs. We do not yet have evidence that *current* Rota remains defective or that its full architecture is a patchwork. The prior SOFT→HARD transition and later OCHRONA scope correction are documented product decisions, not automatically evidence of disobedience.

For Maestro research, the question to test is whether a compact, mechanically checkable *impact obligation* linking an owner invariant to all affected user flows (automatic plan, independent validation, manual edit, later replan, 24h components, cross-month history, regime scope) could have flagged the omissions before merge, **without creating a second full spec or a bureaucracy**. No such mechanism is adopted by this note.
